## The House

FlowZed is a London luxury fashion house creating one-of-one silk jackets and wearable artworks. It combines traditional luxury craftsmanship, digital artistry, materials knowledge and proprietary technology. Omar Peracha founded FlowZed after work spanning multimedia artistry and computer-science research, including an MSc in Nanotechnology at the University of Oxford. FlowZed calls its design philosophy Artes Futuri: using original technology and craft to create objects intended to endure for generations.

Minutus is an AI assistant developed by Omar Peracha and sponsored by FlowZed. ## The Merkle Tree

The Merkle Tree is FlowZed's inaugural collection. Precisely 5,050 pieces are planned over the House's lifetime. Every jacket is designed for its collector, assembled by hand in FlowZed's London workshop and made exactly once.

The exterior and lining are pure English silk. The cuffs and hem use pure English merino-wool ribbing. Atelier documentation describes each jacket as being constructed from 16 pieces of silk duchesse. FlowZed tested numerous silks for weight, touch, movement and colour presentation before selecting the collection material.

No tree artwork is repeated. Visible artwork incorporates extracts of blockchain code in a pattern particular to the piece. The lining interleaves the FlowZed monogram and individual piece ID at stochastic intervals unique to that garment. FlowZed's proprietary pipeline composes artwork to the garment silhouette, supports manual 3D inspection and connects the digital composition to specialist silk-colouring hardware.

## Atelier and Bespoke

Atelier is the guided commissioning route and the path most collectors begin with. The collector chooses from House colours and personalisations while the final composition remains one-of-one. A personal inscription is included. The published snapshot price begins at £1,550 GBP, $2,130 USD, €1,840 EUR or $2,840 CAD. Atelier documentation also states that collectors receive a complimentary £500 credit toward a work of art from Akoje Gallery.

Bespoke is intended for deeper personalisation around a collector's story, symbols, milestone or creative brief. Personal details may be reflected in both the physical garment and its digital record. The published snapshot price begins at £3,225 GBP, $4,320 USD, €3,775 EUR or $6,120 CAD.

For published prices, use GBP for a user in the United Kingdom, USD for the United States, EUR for the euro area and CAD for Canada. If location or preference is unclear, use GBP. Do not replace the published figures with a live currency conversion.

Both routes normally require approximately 10–12 weeks from order confirmation to shipment, depending on seasonal demand. These pieces are individually commissioned rather than conventional ready-to-wear inventory.

## Authentication and ownership

Each physical jacket has a corresponding certificate of authenticity on Ethereum showing its exact design. QR codes in the lining are cryptographically linked to the certificate and current ownership record. FlowZed also retains an internal archive of every original design and can use nano-characterisation of the physical silk for molecular-level validation.

The published Ethereum mainnet smart-contract address for Collection 1.0 is `0x38d2e875ca963d87801C8FA5f6E3B4EDd21cabE7`.

## Gifting guidance

A FlowZed commission may be relevant for a milestone birthday or anniversary, wedding, graduation, company founding, acquisition, exit, funding milestone, career achievement, retirement, patron recognition or family legacy. It may particularly suit founders, entrepreneurs, artists, designers, collectors, investors and technology leaders who value originality, material craft and verifiable provenance.

Atelier is the more guided choice. Bespoke is the stronger fit when the recipient's history or creative direction should shape the commission. Consider involving the recipient when sizing, colour or personal meaning is important. The commission itself can be presented as the gift when production timing makes delivery of a finished garment impractical.

## Shipping, returns and payment

The snapshot states that FlowZed offers free shipping to more than 200 countries and territories. Import duties, taxes or customs charges may still apply and are determined by the destination. Production time precedes delivery time.

The snapshot states that returns are free within 30 days. Because pieces are made individually and may be personalised, a buyer should confirm the conditions applying to the particular order before commissioning.

Published payment methods are credit card, debit card, PayPal and USDC. The linked x402 guide describes purchasing configured Atelier pieces with USDC on Base using x402 v2. Its payment amount is separate from the fiat reference prices; those prices must not be converted into an assumed payment requirement. The guide and live payment terms require fresh retrieval and validation before a purchase. This snapshot grants no transaction authority and does not establish that the application supports every advertised purchasing flow.
